# 🎓 ATTENDLY — Know Where You Stand.

A simple, student-friendly attendance tracker designed to help college students monitor their attendance, plan lectures, and stay on track with their academic goals.

🔗 **Live Website:** https://adityyapawar.github.io/attendly/

## 📌 About ATTENDLY

Managing college attendance shouldn't be complicated.

Students often have questions like:

- 📊 What is my current attendance percentage?
- 📅 How many lectures can I safely miss?
- 🎯 How many lectures do I need to attend to improve my attendance?
- ⚠️ Which subjects need more attention?

**ATTENDLY** brings these answers together in one clean, easy-to-use platform.

## ✨ Features

- 📊 **Dashboard:** Get a quick overview of your overall and subject-wise attendance.
- 📚 **Subject Management:** Add, edit, and delete subjects.
- ✅ **Attendance Tracking:** Mark lectures as present or absent.
- 🏖️ **Safe Leave Calculator:** Find out how many lectures you can miss while maintaining your attendance target.
- 📈 **Recovery Calculator:** Calculate how many lectures you need to attend to improve your percentage.
- 🧮 **What-if Calculator:** Understand how future attendance decisions affect your percentage.
- 💡 **Attendance Insights:** Identify subjects that need attention.
- 🎯 **Custom Attendance Target:** Set your preferred attendance target. The default is 80%.
- 🔒 **Local Data Storage:** Your profile and attendance records are saved locally on your device.

## 🛠️ Built With

- 🌐 HTML5
- 🎨 CSS3
- ⚡ Vanilla JavaScript
- 💾 Browser LocalStorage

Lightweight, simple, and built without unnecessary frameworks.

## 🔐 Privacy

ATTENDLY stores your profile and attendance records locally in your browser using LocalStorage. Your attendance data is not stored in a central ATTENDLY database.

Your data stays on your device and may not be available if you clear your browser data or switch devices.
